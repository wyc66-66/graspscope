feat: OpenVocab-GraspGate closed-loop deployability audit

Cross-domain instantiation of the OpenGate methodology for open-vocabulary
robotic grasping: perception profile -> closed-loop GraspEnv -> coverage
sweep -> deployment gate.

alpha >= 0.653 for a 25% failure bound; real-scene anchor 72% success.
750 synth + 149 real scenes, YOLO-World perception, 5-seed aggregation.
Includes arXiv-style technical report (docs/paper/graspgate) and live
dashboard (src/opengate/ui).
